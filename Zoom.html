About Zoom

One platform for modern communication.

Zoom is a communication and collaboration platform best known for its video conferencing capabilities. It allows individuals, businesses, educators and organizations to connect through online meetings from different locations.

Zoom has expanded beyond video calls. Zoom Workplace brings together meetings, Team Chat, phone services, calendars, whiteboards, notes and other productivity features in one connected environment.

Whether you are hosting a business presentation, attending an online class or collaborating with remote colleagues, Zoom provides tools designed to make digital communication more convenient.

Why People Choose Zoom

  • Connect with colleagues and customers remotely.
  • Share your screen and present documents.
  • Host meetings for teams, classes and events.
  • Use chat and collaboration tools alongside meetings.
  • Access supported AI features for meeting productivity.
  • Join meetings from supported computers and mobile devices.

A closer look at how video communication and workplace tools support modern collaboration.

What Makes Zoom a Popular Video Conferencing Platform?

Online communication has become an important part of modern business, education and personal collaboration. Zoom makes it possible for people to communicate without being in the same physical location. Participants can join a meeting using a supported computer, smartphone, tablet or web browser.

Professional Meetings and Presentations

Zoom supports professional meetings where participants can communicate through audio and video, share presentations and discuss important topics. Hosts can manage participants and use meeting controls to create a more organized experience.

Businesses can use it for team discussions, customer meetings, interviews, training sessions and presentations. Educators can also use online meetings for virtual lessons and group discussions.

Screen Sharing and Collaboration

Screen sharing is useful when a conversation requires more than spoken explanations. A presenter can show slides, demonstrate software, review documents or walk colleagues through a process. Depending on the meeting configuration, participants may also use annotation and other collaboration options.

AI Features and Meeting Productivity

Zoom AI Companion adds AI-powered assistance to supported Zoom products. Depending on account eligibility, settings and regional availability, features can include meeting summaries, AI questions, chat summaries and help with follow-up tasks.

These tools can help participants catch up on discussions and organize information. Users should review AI-generated content for accuracy before relying on it for important decisions.

Zoom for Remote and Hybrid Work

Remote teams need reliable ways to communicate, share updates and maintain working relationships. Zoom provides meetings, chat and other collaboration features that can help teams stay connected across different locations and time zones.

Security and Meeting Controls

Zoom provides meeting security and host-management features, including options for meeting passwords, waiting rooms, participant permissions and other controls. Available settings depend on the account, product and administrator configuration. Hosts should review their settings before sharing sensitive information.

Is Zoom Free or Paid?

Zoom Workplace offers a free Basic plan and several paid subscriptions. The Basic plan currently includes meetings with up to 100 participants and a 40-minute meeting limit. Paid plans provide longer meeting durations and additional capabilities. Business and Enterprise options offer features designed for larger organizations. Pricing, availability and included features can change, so check Zoom's official pricing page before choosing a subscription.

Compatibility

What do you need to use Zoom?

Zoom can be used on supported Windows, macOS and Linux computers, as well as compatible mobile devices and web browsers.

For a better meeting experience, use a stable internet connection, a working microphone and speakers, and a webcam if you want to participate with video.

Always check Zoom's official system requirements because supported operating systems and app versions may change.

Basic Equipment Checklist

  • A supported desktop, laptop, tablet or smartphone.
  • A stable broadband or mobile internet connection.
  • Speakers or headphones.
  • A microphone for speaking during meetings.
  • A webcam for video participation.
  • A supported app version or modern web browser.
Frequently asked questions

Everything you should know about Zoom.

What is Zoom used for?

Zoom is used for video conferencing, online meetings, webinars, screen sharing, team messaging and workplace collaboration.

Is Zoom free to use?

Yes. Zoom offers a free Basic plan with limits on meeting duration and some features. Paid subscriptions provide additional capabilities.

Can I use Zoom on my phone?

Yes. Zoom offers mobile apps for supported iOS and Android devices. You can join meetings, communicate and use supported collaboration features.

Can I share my screen during a Zoom meeting?

Yes. Screen sharing allows participants with permission to present their desktop, application windows and other supported content.

Does Zoom provide meeting recordings?

Zoom supports meeting recording. Available recording options, storage and limits depend on your subscription, device and administrator settings.

What is Zoom AI Companion?

Zoom AI Companion is a set of AI-powered features that can assist with tasks such as meeting summaries, questions and communication workflows. Eligibility and availability vary.

Where can I download Zoom?

Download Zoom from its official download page: zoom.us/download .

Is Zoom suitable for businesses?

Yes. Zoom Workplace offers communication and collaboration products for individuals, small businesses and larger organizations. The best plan depends on your team's needs.
