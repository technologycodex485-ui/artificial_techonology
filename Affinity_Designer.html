What Is Keka?

Keka is a modern human resource management and payroll software platform that helps businesses manage their workforce and organize important HR activities. It brings multiple employee management processes together in one digital environment.

From employee information and attendance tracking to payroll processing and performance management, Keka provides tools that help HR departments organize their everyday responsibilities more efficiently.

Why Is Keka HR Software Important?

Managing employees manually can become challenging as a company grows. HR teams need to maintain employee records, calculate salaries, monitor attendance, manage leave requests and coordinate recruitment.

Keka aims to simplify these processes by bringing important HR functions into a connected platform. This can help organizations reduce repetitive administrative work and improve access to employee information.

Main Features of Keka

Keka offers several tools that support different areas of human resource management. Some of its important features include:

01. Payroll Management

Payroll tools help businesses organize salary calculations, deductions, payslips and payroll processing activities.

02. Attendance Management

Businesses can manage employee attendance, working hours, shifts and leave information through centralized workflows.

03. Employee Management

Employee records, documents, organizational information and other important details can be managed in one place.

04. Recruitment and Hiring

Recruitment tools support candidate tracking, interview coordination, hiring workflows and employee onboarding.

05. Performance Management

Performance tools support employee goals, reviews, continuous feedback and performance evaluation.

06. Employee Self-Service

Employees can access relevant HR information and manage supported requests through self-service features.
