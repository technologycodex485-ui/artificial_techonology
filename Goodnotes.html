Get to know Goodnotes

What Is Goodnotes?

Goodnotes is a digital note-taking and document organization application designed to make writing, learning, and planning more convenient. It gives users a workspace where they can create handwritten notebooks, type structured documents, annotate PDFs, and organize important information.

Instead of carrying multiple physical notebooks and folders, users can keep many types of documents together digitally. Its writing tools make it useful for people who enjoy taking notes by hand, while text documents and whiteboards support more flexible ways of working.

Goodnotes also offers AI-powered tools on supported plans. Depending on the subscription and device, these can help users understand their notes, work with documents, and improve their study or productivity routines.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Goodnotes mainly used for?

Goodnotes is used for digital handwriting, typed notes, PDF annotation, document organization, planning, and supported study and AI features.

Is Goodnotes free to use?

Goodnotes offers a free plan with limits on documents, storage, and selected features. Paid plans provide additional capabilities. Check the official pricing information for current details in your region.

Can I use Goodnotes on Windows and Android?

Yes. Goodnotes is available on Apple devices, Android, Windows, and the web. Specific features and syncing options depend on your device and plan.

Can Goodnotes open and annotate PDFs?

Yes. You can import supported PDF documents and use available annotation tools to highlight, write, and mark up their contents.

Does Goodnotes include AI features?

Yes. Goodnotes offers AI tools for eligible plans. Available capabilities and usage limits vary by subscription and platform.

Can I use Goodnotes without an internet connection?

Offline access is supported for many note-taking activities. Cloud synchronization and online services require an internet connection.
