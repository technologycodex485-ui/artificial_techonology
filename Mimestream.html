What Is Mimestream?

Mimestream is a dedicated email application for macOS that brings Gmail into a convenient desktop environment. It combines Gmail functionality with an interface designed specifically for Mac users.

Whether you use email for business, education, or everyday communication, Mimestream provides a focused way to manage your inbox.

Powerful Features

01. Mac Experience

Enjoy an interface designed to feel natural and comfortable on macOS.

02. Gmail Integration

Access and manage your Gmail conversations from your desktop.

03. Inbox Management

Keep email conversations organized and find important messages easily.

04. Multiple Accounts

Manage multiple Gmail accounts through one convenient interface.

Why Choose Mimestream?

For people who spend much of their day using Gmail on a Mac, a dedicated email application can make everyday communication more convenient. Mimestream focuses on bringing Gmail features into a streamlined desktop experience.
