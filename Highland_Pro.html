What Is Highland Pro for Mac?

Highland Pro is a writing application for macOS that focuses on screenwriting and long-form creative writing. It provides a streamlined workspace where writers can develop scripts, organize their thoughts, and concentrate on storytelling.

Instead of relying entirely on traditional word-processing layouts, Highland uses a writing-focused approach that helps users work with screenplay formatting and other creative document styles.
