Complete software guide

What Is 1Password for Mac?

1Password is a password manager that helps individuals, families, and businesses store and manage sensitive digital information. It provides a central place for login credentials, payment details, secure notes, passkeys, and other important information.

Instead of remembering a different password for every online account, you can use 1Password to generate and store unique credentials. When you need to sign in, supported apps and browser extensions can help fill in your saved information.

Why Use 1Password on macOS?

The Mac application provides a dedicated environment for accessing your secure information. You can organize items into categories and vaults, search for saved credentials, and use supported autofill tools when signing in to websites and applications.

1Password also works with browser extensions and other supported devices, helping you access your information across your digital routine. Its features make it useful for people who manage many online accounts or need a structured way to protect sensitive information.

Security reminder: A password manager helps you organize and protect credentials, but it cannot guarantee that every account or device is secure. Use a strong account password, protect your devices, enable available security protections, and keep your software updated.

How Does 1Password Work?

1Password stores your information in encrypted vaults. You sign in to your account to access your saved items, and the application provides tools for creating, organizing, and using them.

Its security model uses end-to-end encryption and a Secret Key alongside your account password. This additional layer helps protect your account information. Keep your account recovery details and Secret Key information safe.

Protect Your Accounts With Unique Passwords

Reusing passwords creates a security risk. If one website experiences a breach, a reused password may put other accounts at risk too. 1Password's password generator helps create different credentials for different services.

You can save those passwords in your vault instead of writing them down or trying to remember every combination. This makes it easier to maintain better password habits without adding unnecessary friction to your daily routine.

Passkeys and Modern Sign-in Methods

Passkeys are a passwordless sign-in technology supported by an increasing number of websites and applications. Rather than entering a traditional password, you authenticate using a supported device or authentication method.

1Password supports saving and using passkeys for compatible services. Availability depends on the website, operating system, browser, and application version.

Watchtower: Understand Your Password Security

Watchtower helps identify security issues connected to information stored in your vault. It can alert you about weak or reused passwords, known breaches, and other account-related security concerns.

These alerts can help you decide which accounts need attention. For example, you might update an old password, replace a reused credential, or enable stronger authentication on a supported website.

Secure Sharing for Families and Teams

Sometimes you need to share a Wi-Fi password, household login, payment information, or work credential with another person. 1Password provides shared vaults and secure item-sharing options to help control how information is shared.

You can also create sharing links with settings such as expiration and access restrictions. Always check who can access a shared item and remove access when it is no longer required.

Is 1Password Worth Using?

1Password can be a useful choice for people who manage multiple online accounts and want a dedicated place for credentials and sensitive information. Its combination of password generation, autofill, passkey support, Watchtower, and secure sharing makes it more than a simple password storage tool.

However, it is a subscription-based service rather than a completely free password manager. Before subscribing, compare its plans, features, and cost with your needs and other available password-management options.

Plans and subscriptions

Is 1Password free?

1Password is a subscription-based password manager. Personal and family plans offer a free trial, while different business plans are available for organizations.

Individual Plan

Personal use

Designed for one person who wants to store passwords, generate strong credentials, use autofill, share selected items securely, and monitor account security.

Families & Business

Shared security

Family and business plans provide additional options for multiple users, shared vaults, administration, and managing access to sensitive information.

1Password offers a 14-day free trial on eligible personal and family plans. Subscription prices and promotions vary by billing period and region. Check the official pricing page for current details.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about 1Password for Mac.

What is 1Password used for?

1Password is used to securely store and manage passwords, passkeys, credit cards, secure notes, and other sensitive digital information.

Is 1Password available for Mac?

Yes. 1Password provides a dedicated application for macOS, along with browser extensions and applications for other supported platforms.

Is 1Password free?

1Password is a paid subscription service. Eligible Individual and Families plans offer a 14-day free trial.

Does 1Password support passkeys?

Yes. 1Password supports saving and using passkeys for compatible websites and services.

Can 1Password automatically fill passwords?

Yes. Supported browser extensions and macOS autofill integrations can help fill saved passwords and other information in compatible websites and apps.

What is 1Password Watchtower?

Watchtower helps identify security concerns such as weak or reused passwords and known breaches associated with saved accounts.

Can I share passwords with family members?

Yes. 1Password Families supports shared vaults and access management for family members. Each member can also keep private information in a personal vault.

Which macOS version does 1Password require?

1Password 8 for Mac requires macOS Monterey 12 or later. Check the official system requirements for current compatibility information and feature-specific requirements.
