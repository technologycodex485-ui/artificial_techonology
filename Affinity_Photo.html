01 / Introduction

What Is Affinity Photo?

Affinity Photo is a name widely associated with professional image editing and digital photography. Its familiar tools include layers, masks, RAW processing, retouching, color adjustments, and detailed image compositing.

The Affinity product has since evolved. The current all-in-one Affinity application combines pixel-based photo editing with vector design and page layout tools. Instead of treating these creative disciplines as separate applications, it brings them together in one customizable workspace.

A flexible workspace for creative projects

Photographers can refine images, designers can combine raster and vector elements, and creative professionals can prepare layouts without constantly moving between separate applications. Its non-destructive editing approach helps preserve original content while allowing adjustments to be revisited.

Whether you are familiar with the earlier Affinity Photo application or exploring Affinity for the first time, the platform offers a broad collection of tools for detailed creative work.

07 / Frequently asked questions

Affinity Photo FAQs

Helpful answers to common questions about Affinity and its photo editing capabilities.

Is Affinity Photo free?

The current all-in-one Affinity application is free to use, including its core photo editing, vector, and layout tools. You need a Canva account to download and activate it.

Is Affinity Photo still a separate application?

The current Affinity product combines the former photo, vector, and page layout workflows in one application. The older Affinity V2 applications are separate legacy products and should not be confused with the new app.

Can I edit RAW photographs with Affinity?

Yes. Affinity includes RAW development tools that allow photographers to refine exposure, tones, color, and other image properties.

Does Affinity support layers and masks?

Yes. Layer-based editing, masks, adjustment layers, and blending options are important parts of its photo editing workflow.

Does Affinity work on Windows and Mac?

The current desktop application is available for Windows and macOS. Check the official download page for current operating-system requirements and installer options.

Do I need Canva Pro to use Affinity?

No. A free Canva account is enough to use the core Affinity application. Canva premium plans are required for certain optional Canva AI features.

Can beginners use Affinity?

Yes. Beginners can start with basic image adjustments and gradually learn layers, masks, retouching, and more advanced creative techniques.
