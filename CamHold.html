01 / Introduction

What Is CamHold?

A virtual camera application designed to give Mac users flexible control over their webcam output during online meetings.

CamHold is a macOS utility developed by Bryan Julio. It creates a virtual camera that can be selected inside compatible video calling applications such as Zoom, Google Meet, FaceTime, and Microsoft Teams.

Rather than simply turning your webcam off, CamHold lets you control the video feed sent to the meeting application. You can hold the last frame, display a short recorded loop, hide the picture, or intentionally reduce its visual quality.

The app is designed for people who regularly participate in online meetings and want convenient camera controls without navigating several settings menus. Its controls are available through the menu bar, keyboard shortcuts, and supported macOS Control Center integration.

CamHold is made specifically for Mac, requires macOS 14 or later, and uses a camera extension that needs approval in macOS during initial setup.

03 / Camera Controls

Four Ways to Manage Your Video

CamHold provides four primary camera modes or effects. You can select the one that suits your current situation.

MODE 01

Freeze

Keeps the most recent frame on screen. Useful when you need to pause your live video temporarily.

OPTION + COMMAND + F
MODE 02

Loop

Plays a short, user-recorded video clip repeatedly. The clip contains video without audio.

OPTION + COMMAND + L
MODE 03

Hide

Replaces the outgoing video with black or a selected picture instead of showing your live camera.

OPTION + COMMAND + H
EFFECT 04

Degrade

Gradually reduces image quality using blur or pixelation at different intensity levels.

OPTION + COMMAND + B
04 / Getting Started

How to Set Up CamHold

The initial setup involves installing the app, approving the virtual camera extension, and selecting CamHold inside your preferred meeting application.

STEP 01

Install the App

Download CamHold from its official Mac App Store listing and open the application on a compatible Mac.

STEP 02

Approve Camera Extension

Follow the setup instructions and approve the camera extension when macOS requests permission.

STEP 03

Select CamHold

Open your meeting app's camera settings and choose CamHold as the video input device.

05 / Privacy

A Local-First Camera Utility

🔒

Designed With On-Device Processing

It does not require an account or use analytics. The short Loop clip is stored locally when you choose to record it. Review the current privacy policy before installing any camera software.

07 / Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About CamHold

What is CamHold used for?

CamHold is a virtual camera utility for Mac that lets users freeze, loop, hide, or apply visual effects to their webcam feed during compatible video calls.

Does CamHold work on Windows?

No. CamHold is currently a Mac application and requires macOS 14 or later.

Which video calling apps support CamHold?

It works with Zoom, Google Meet, FaceTime, Microsoft Teams, and other applications that allow users to select a camera input.

Does CamHold record live video?

According to its privacy policy, live video is processed locally and is not recorded. A short Loop clip is recorded only when you explicitly choose to create one.

Is CamHold free?

Yes. A free version is available. It includes the main camera modes and adds a small watermark. Pro is an optional one-time purchase.

Does CamHold control microphone audio?

No. CamHold controls the video feed, not your microphone. Use your meeting application's mute control whenever you need to stop sending audio.

Where can I get CamHold?

CamHold is distributed through the Mac App Store. Visit its official website for product information and the current installation link.
