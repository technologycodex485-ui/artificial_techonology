What Is TeraBox?

TeraBox is a cloud storage service that allows users to upload, store, manage, and share digital files online. Developed by Flextech Inc., the platform provides cloud-based storage for photos, videos, documents, and other files.

Instead of keeping every file on your phone or computer, you can upload selected content to your cloud account and access it through supported devices and web browsers.

TeraBox is designed for people who need additional online storage, convenient file sharing, and a way to keep important digital content organized.

Cloud Storage Made Convenient: Store your digital content online, access files from supported devices, and manage your collection without depending entirely on local device storage.

How Much Storage Does TeraBox Offer?

TeraBox advertises up to 1 TB (1024 GB) of cloud storage with its basic free plan. This provides substantial room for users who want to store personal files online.

Paid plans offer additional capacity and features. Depending on the subscription, these can include larger uploads, faster transfers, improved video playback, and additional AI tools.

Storage limits, file restrictions, and subscription benefits can change, so check the official pricing page before choosing a plan.

Automatic Backup and File Synchronization

One of the useful aspects of TeraBox is its backup functionality. Users can enable supported backup options to upload selected photos, videos, and folders to cloud storage.

This can help keep important files available when switching devices or freeing up local storage space.

Supported synchronization features can also help keep files updated across devices connected to the same account.
