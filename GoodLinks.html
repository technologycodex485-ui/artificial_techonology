What Is GoodLinks?

GoodLinks is a read-later application and bookmark manager developed by Ngoc Luu. It helps users save interesting web pages and articles so they can read them whenever they have free time.

In today's digital world, people discover useful information across websites, social media, news platforms, blogs, and online publications. However, finding enough time to read everything immediately can be difficult.

GoodLinks provides a convenient solution by allowing users to collect articles in one place. Instead of keeping dozens of browser tabs open, you can save interesting content and return to it whenever you are ready.

"Save the things worth reading, organize your knowledge, and enjoy every article without unnecessary distractions."

A Better Reading Experience

One of GoodLinks' main attractions is its distraction-free reading environment. The app extracts article content and presents it in a cleaner format, allowing readers to focus on the information that matters.

Users can customize reading appearance with different themes, fonts, spacing, and text sizes. This makes it easier to create a comfortable reading experience for different situations.

GoodLinks also supports highlights, notes, and article summaries through supported Apple Intelligence or external AI providers.

Why Use GoodLinks?

GoodLinks can be helpful for people who regularly discover useful online information but do not always have time to read it immediately.

Students can save educational resources, professionals can collect industry articles, and researchers can organize useful reading materials for future reference.

Its search, tagging, highlighting, and note-taking features can also make it easier to revisit important information instead of searching through browser history.

Privacy and convenience: GoodLinks does not require users to create a separate account. It uses iCloud for supported synchronization features and emphasizes keeping saved reading information private.

GoodLinks for iPhone, iPad, and Mac

GoodLinks is a universal Apple application available for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. This makes it convenient for people who use multiple Apple devices throughout their day.

You can save an article while browsing on your iPhone and continue reading later on another supported device using synchronization.

Its device compatibility makes GoodLinks a useful option for people who want a consistent reading and bookmarking workflow.
