About Prime Video

A world of entertainment in one streaming destination

Amazon Prime Video is a video-on-demand and digital streaming service operated by Amazon. It gives viewers access to a wide selection of movies, television shows, documentaries and exclusive Amazon Original productions.

Depending on the country and subscription, users can watch selected titles included with their membership. Other content may be available to rent or purchase, while additional streaming services can be accessed through optional channel subscriptions.

Prime Video is designed to make entertainment accessible through supported smart TVs, computers, smartphones, tablets and streaming media devices. Its available catalog and features can differ depending on the viewer's location.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about Amazon Prime Video

Learn more about subscriptions, supported devices, downloads and streaming options.

1. What is Amazon Prime Video?

Amazon Prime Video is a streaming service operated by Amazon that offers movies, television shows, documentaries, original productions and other digital video content.

2. Is Prime Video included with Amazon Prime?

In eligible countries, an Amazon Prime membership includes access to selected Prime Video titles. Membership benefits and availability differ by region.

3. Can I watch Prime Video on my smartphone?

Yes. The Prime Video app is available for supported Android and iOS devices. Device compatibility and playback quality may vary.

4. Can I download movies from Prime Video?

Many eligible titles can be downloaded through supported mobile applications. Download availability, viewing periods and restrictions depend on the title and subscription.

5. Does Prime Video offer original movies and series?

Yes. Prime Video features Amazon Original productions, including original films, television series and documentaries.

6. Do all Prime Video movies come with a membership?

No. Some titles are included with a membership, while others require separate rental or purchase payments. Optional channel subscriptions can also cost extra.

7. Can I watch Prime Video on my television?

Yes. The app is supported on selected smart TVs and compatible streaming devices. Check Amazon's official supported-device information for your model.

8. Does Prime Video have subtitles?

Many titles offer subtitles, captions or alternative audio tracks. Available languages and accessibility features depend on the specific content.

9. Is Prime Video available everywhere?

Prime Video is available in many countries and territories, but the content catalog, membership options and features vary according to location.

10. Does Prime Video include advertisements?

Advertising policies depend on the market and content type. An eligible ad-free option may be available in some locations, although certain live or ad-supported programming can still include advertisements.
