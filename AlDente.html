What Is AlDente?

AlDente is a battery management application for macOS that gives MacBook users additional control over how their device charges. Its main purpose is to let users set a maximum battery charge percentage instead of always allowing the battery to charge to 100%.

This can be useful for people who frequently keep their MacBook connected to a power adapter, such as professionals working at a desk, students attending online classes, and users with fixed workstation setups.

AlDente places its controls in the macOS menu bar, making it convenient to view and adjust charging settings without navigating through multiple system menus.

Good to know: A charge limit is a battery-management preference, not a guarantee that your battery will never degrade. Battery aging also depends on temperature, usage, charging cycles, and other conditions.

Why Do MacBook Users Consider AlDente?

Modern MacBook batteries use lithium-based battery technology. Battery lifespan is influenced by several factors, including how long the battery stays at a very high charge level and how much heat it experiences.

Users who leave their MacBooks plugged in for long periods may prefer to maintain a lower charge level for everyday desk use. AlDente provides a way to set that preference and manage charging behavior through a dedicated application.

For example, someone who normally works near a power outlet might choose a limit around 80%. Before travelling, that person can temporarily increase the limit to charge the MacBook more fully. The ideal setting depends on individual usage and power needs.

Is AlDente Worth Using?

AlDente can be useful for MacBook owners who want more direct control over charging than their normal workflow provides. Its charge limiter is particularly relevant to people who keep their laptops connected to power for much of the day.

The free version may be sufficient for users who only need a charging limit and basic discharge control. Those interested in scheduling, temperature-related controls, and additional monitoring may want to explore the Pro edition.

However, no third-party utility can stop the natural aging of a lithium-ion battery. AlDente should be considered one part of a broader battery-care routine rather than a guaranteed battery-life solution.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does AlDente do on a MacBook?

AlDente is a macOS battery management application that lets users set a maximum charging percentage and access additional charging controls.

Is AlDente free to use?

Yes. AlDente offers a free edition with core features. AlDente Pro is a paid upgrade with additional tools.

Does AlDente help protect battery health?

It gives users more control over charging behavior, which may help reduce time spent at a very high charge level. Actual battery aging depends on several factors and results are not guaranteed.

Can I set my MacBook charging limit to 80%?

AlDente supports configurable charging limits. On supported Apple silicon MacBooks, enabling limits below 80% may require an additional setting in the app.

Does AlDente work on every Mac?

AlDente is designed for compatible MacBook models, including supported Intel and Apple silicon devices. Check the official compatibility information for your model and macOS version.

What is the difference between AlDente Free and Pro?

Free provides core charge-limiting and discharge features. Pro adds tools such as Sailing Mode, Heat Protection, Calibration Mode, scheduling, and more detailed statistics.

Where should I download AlDente?

For the genuine application, use the official developer website or its verified distribution channels. Check the download source before installing software.
