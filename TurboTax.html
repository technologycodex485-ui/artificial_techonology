Understanding TurboTax

What is TurboTax?

TurboTax is a tax preparation software product developed by Intuit. It helps individuals and eligible businesses prepare tax returns using guided questions, document import features and calculations based on information entered by the user.

Instead of manually completing every tax form, users can answer questions about their income, employment, expenses and personal circumstances. The software uses those answers to help prepare the relevant tax forms.

TurboTax provides different products and assistance options. Depending on the product, users may prepare their own returns, receive help from tax professionals or have an expert handle the preparation process.

What Can TurboTax Help With?

  • Preparing eligible individual tax returns.
  • Entering income and tax document information.
  • Identifying applicable deductions and tax credits.
  • Reviewing tax return information before filing.
  • Accessing eligible expert assistance.
  • Preparing and submitting supported returns electronically.

How Does TurboTax Work?

TurboTax uses an interview-style process to collect information about a user's tax situation. It asks questions about employment, income, family circumstances, expenses and other relevant details. Based on the responses, the software helps determine which sections of the return may need to be completed.

Users can enter information manually or import supported documents. The software then uses the information provided to populate relevant tax forms and calculate figures for review.

Understanding Tax Deductions and Credits

Deductions and tax credits can affect the amount of tax a taxpayer owes or the refund they may receive. TurboTax guides users through questions intended to identify potentially applicable tax benefits.

The deductions and credits available depend on the taxpayer's circumstances and applicable tax rules. Users should provide accurate information and review the eligibility requirements before claiming a tax benefit.

TurboTax for Employees and Freelancers

Different income sources can require different tax forms. Employees may have W-2 income, while independent contractors and freelancers may receive 1099 forms and have business expenses to consider.

TurboTax offers different product levels for different situations. Users with self-employment, investment or rental property income should check which product supports their specific forms and filing requirements.

TurboTax Online and Mobile App

TurboTax offers online tax preparation and a mobile application. Mobile users can access supported features such as capturing tax documents with a phone camera and continuing their work across supported devices.

The exact tools available may differ between the mobile app and online experience. Check the current product details to understand which features are supported on your device.

Can You Get Help from a Tax Professional?

TurboTax offers different assistance options, including products that provide access to tax experts and full-service options where a professional prepares the return.

This can be useful for people who have questions about deductions, income reporting or more complicated tax circumstances. Availability, expert services and fees depend on the selected product and location.

Is TurboTax Safe to Use?

Tax returns contain sensitive financial and personal information. TurboTax provides account and data protection features, but users should also take care when accessing their accounts.

  • Use a strong, unique account password.
  • Enable available multi-factor authentication options.
  • Only access TurboTax through its official website or app.
  • Keep devices and browsers updated.
  • Never share login codes or account credentials.

Who is it for?

Different products for different tax situations.

TurboTax offers multiple filing experiences. The right option depends on the type of income, forms and support a taxpayer needs.

Employees with W-2 Income

Individuals with straightforward employment income may be eligible for the Free Edition, subject to its form and eligibility requirements.

Families and Homeowners

People with eligible deductions, dependents, mortgage interest or other tax considerations may need a product that supports additional tax forms.

Freelancers and Self-Employed Users

Independent contractors and business owners should look for a product that supports self-employment income, business expenses and relevant schedules.

Investors and Property Owners

Users with investment sales, cryptocurrency activity or rental income may need a product designed for more complex tax situations.

Important: Free Filing Has Eligibility Rules

TurboTax Free Edition is not available for every tax situation. Its current US offering is limited to eligible simple Form 1040 returns and specified credits. Other income types and forms may require a paid product. Always review the official eligibility rules and final price before filing.

Country and tax compatibility

Is TurboTax available for every country's taxes?

No. TurboTax products are designed for specific tax jurisdictions. The US and Canadian versions, for example, support different tax systems and government filing processes.

The US TurboTax service is designed for US federal and supported state tax returns. TurboTax Canada is designed for Canadian tax returns and CRA-related filing workflows.

Do not assume that TurboTax prepares Pakistani income tax returns or supports filing with Pakistan's Federal Board of Revenue (FBR). Check the relevant local tax authority and software provider before choosing a tax service.

Before Choosing a Product

  • Confirm which country's tax return you need to file.
  • Check whether the software supports your income types.
  • Review the required tax forms and schedules.
  • Confirm whether electronic filing is available.
  • Check the total cost, including any state filing fees.
Frequently asked questions

Common questions about TurboTax.

What is TurboTax used for?

TurboTax is tax preparation software that helps eligible users prepare tax returns, enter income information, review deductions and credits, and file supported returns.

Is TurboTax free?

TurboTax offers a Free Edition for qualifying simple US tax returns. Other tax situations may require paid products or additional services.

Can I use TurboTax on my phone?

Yes. TurboTax offers mobile apps for supported iOS and Android devices. Some features differ from the online desktop experience.

Does TurboTax support self-employed users?

Yes. TurboTax offers products designed for eligible self-employed and business-related tax situations. Check the current product requirements to confirm that your forms are supported.

Can a tax expert help me with TurboTax?

Yes. Certain TurboTax products offer expert advice, review or full-service tax preparation. These services may involve additional fees and eligibility requirements.

Is TurboTax available outside the United States?

TurboTax has country-specific products, including a Canadian offering. Product availability and tax support vary by country. It should not be assumed to support every country's tax system.

Does TurboTax guarantee a tax refund?

No software can guarantee that every taxpayer will receive a refund. The result depends on income, withholding, deductions, credits and applicable tax rules. Product guarantees have specific terms and conditions.

Where can I access TurboTax?

Visit the official Intuit TurboTax website: turbotax.intuit.com .
