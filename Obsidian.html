Complete overview

What Is Obsidian for Mac?

Obsidian is a note-taking and knowledge management application designed to help people organize information in a flexible, interconnected system. Rather than keeping every thought in an isolated document, Obsidian lets you create links between notes and develop a network of related knowledge.

Its foundation is the vault: a folder on your computer containing your Markdown notes and related files. You can create separate vaults for personal notes, academic research, work projects, documentation, or creative ideas.

Why Choose Obsidian on macOS?

Mac users who work with research, writing, planning, or large collections of information may appreciate Obsidian's local-first approach. Your notes remain available on your device, including when you are offline. You can also use other tools to access the underlying Markdown files.

The application is suitable for people who want more control over their note organization than a traditional folder-based notebook may provide. Its linking tools make it easier to connect related information and revisit older ideas.

One important distinction: Obsidian stores your vault locally by default. Synchronizing notes between devices is a separate choice, using Obsidian Sync or another compatible synchronization method.

How Obsidian Helps With Everyday Work

Obsidian can become a central workspace for different types of information. Students can organize lecture notes and research. Writers can connect outlines, drafts, and references. Developers can maintain technical documentation, project notes, and useful code explanations.

You can also create daily notes, maintain reading lists, document meetings, organize personal goals, or build a long-term reference library. Community plugins can add specialized functionality, although the best setup depends on your needs.

Is Obsidian Private?

Obsidian's local-first design means your notes are stored on your device rather than automatically uploaded to an Obsidian-hosted account. This gives you control over where your files live. However, privacy also depends on your device security, backups, cloud storage choices, and any third-party plugins or services you decide to use.

Obsidian Sync and Publish

Obsidian itself is free to use. Optional paid services are available for people who need additional capabilities. Obsidian Sync helps synchronize vaults across devices and offers end-to-end encryption. Obsidian Publish lets you turn selected notes into a website.

These services are optional. You can also explore other syncing approaches, but it is important to configure them carefully and maintain backups to protect your notes.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers for people discovering Obsidian for Mac.

What is Obsidian used for?

Obsidian is used for note-taking, personal knowledge management, research, journaling, writing, project planning, and creating interconnected collections of information.

Is Obsidian free for Mac users?

Yes. The core Obsidian application is free. Optional services such as Sync and Publish require payment.

Does Obsidian work offline?

Yes. Your local vault and notes remain accessible without an internet connection. Online access is needed for services that depend on the internet.

Can I sync Obsidian notes between devices?

Yes. You can use Obsidian Sync or configure another compatible file synchronization method. Follow the official guidance to reduce the risk of conflicts or data loss.

Does Obsidian support plugins?

Yes. Obsidian offers core plugins and a community plugin ecosystem that can add extra features and customize your workspace.

Can I publish my notes online?

Yes. Obsidian Publish is an optional service that allows you to publish selected notes as a website.

Are Obsidian notes locked into a special file format?

No. Obsidian primarily uses Markdown text files, which can also be opened and edited with other compatible text editors.
