What is Slack?

A central place for workplace communication.

Slack is a collaboration platform designed to make workplace communication more organized. Instead of relying on scattered email conversations, teams can use channels, direct messages, calls and connected apps to keep work moving.

Whether a company works remotely, in an office or across multiple locations, Slack gives its members a shared digital workspace. Conversations can be organized around departments, projects, clients or specific topics, making it easier to find the right information and involve the right people.

Slack is useful for more than sending messages. Teams can share documents, discuss ideas, coordinate tasks, connect external services and use collaboration tools without constantly switching between separate communication channels.

How Slack supports communication, collaboration and everyday work.

How Slack Makes Team Communication Easier

Good communication is an important part of successful teamwork. When updates are spread across emails, meetings and different applications, employees can lose track of decisions and deadlines. Slack helps bring many of these conversations into a shared workspace.

Keep Projects Organized with Channels

Channels give teams a dedicated place to discuss specific work. A marketing team might create channels for campaigns, while developers might use separate channels for product releases, bug reports and technical discussions. Members can join the conversations relevant to their responsibilities.

Collaborate Beyond Simple Messaging

Slack supports more than text conversations. Depending on the subscription and workspace settings, teams can use huddles, audio and video clips, canvases, lists and file sharing to coordinate their work.

Connect the Tools Your Team Already Uses

Integrations can bring updates from other services into Slack. This can help teams follow project activity, receive alerts and reduce the need to move between multiple applications. Available integrations and capabilities depend on the app and subscription.

Find Information and Catch Up

Search helps users locate conversations and shared information. Some paid plans also include AI-powered features designed to summarize discussions and make it easier to catch up on activity. Access varies by plan and workspace configuration.

Does Slack Have a Free Plan?

Yes. Slack offers a Free plan as well as paid subscriptions such as Pro, Business+ and Enterprise+. Plan limits and included features differ. For example, the Free plan has limited message history and app integrations, while paid plans provide additional collaboration, administration and AI capabilities. Check the official pricing page for current prices and regional availability.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions about Slack.

What is Slack used for?

Slack is used for workplace messaging, team collaboration, project discussions, file sharing, online conversations and connecting supported business applications.

Is Slack free to use?

Slack offers a Free plan with limits on features such as message history, huddles and integrations. Paid plans provide additional capabilities. Check Slack's official pricing page for current details.

Can I use Slack on a mobile device?

Yes. Slack provides mobile apps for supported iOS and Android devices, along with desktop and browser access.

Can Slack connect with other applications?

Yes. Slack supports integrations with many third-party applications. The number and availability of integrations depend on your subscription and workspace settings.

Is Slack suitable for small businesses?

Slack can be useful for small businesses that want organized team communication. A small team can begin with the Free plan and evaluate paid features as its needs grow.

Does Slack support video meetings?

Slack Huddles support audio and video collaboration, including screen sharing. Participant limits and available capabilities vary by subscription.

Where can I download Slack?

Visit the official Slack website to access its supported desktop and mobile downloads: slack.com/downloads .
