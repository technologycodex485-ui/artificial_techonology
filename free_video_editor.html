Top Recommendations

5 Best Free Video Editors for Mac

Here are five popular options worth considering. Each offers a different editing experience, so you can select the one that matches your creative workflow.

01

DaVinci Resolve

DaVinci Resolve is a powerful choice for creators who want professional editing tools without an upfront payment. It combines video editing, color grading, visual effects, and audio production in one application. Its free edition supports Ultra HD output, with format and feature limitations compared with the paid Studio version.

Best Overall
02

Apple iMovie

iMovie is an excellent starting point for Mac beginners. Its clean interface makes it easy to arrange clips, add titles, apply transitions, include music, and create polished home movies or simple YouTube videos. It is free for supported Apple devices.

Best for Beginners
03

Shotcut

Shotcut is a free, open-source video editor with a flexible timeline and broad format support. It includes filters, audio tools, and multi-format editing options. It is a practical choice for users who want desktop editing without a subscription.

Best Open-Source Option
04

Kdenlive

Kdenlive offers a multi-track editing workflow with transitions, effects, titles, and proxy editing. It is suitable for creators who want more timeline control and prefer open-source software. Check the current macOS installer and system requirements before installing.

Best for Multi-Track Editing
05

CapCut

CapCut is designed for quick and engaging social media content. Its templates, effects, text tools, and caption features can help creators prepare short videos for platforms such as TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts. Some assets and features require payment, so check the export conditions before finishing a project.

Best for Social Media
Getting Started

How to Choose the Right Video Editor

Before downloading an editor, think about the kind of videos you make. A beginner creating family videos may not need the same tools as a YouTube creator or independent filmmaker.

A Simple Selection Guide

  1. For simple editing: Start with Apple iMovie.
  2. For professional features: Try DaVinci Resolve.
  3. For open-source software: Explore Shotcut or Kdenlive.
  4. For short social videos: Consider CapCut.
  5. For demanding projects: Check your Mac's memory, storage, and processor requirements.

Tip: Test a short project and export before editing a long video. This helps you check performance, file compatibility, and output quality.

Mac Editing Tips

What to Check Before Installing

Video editing can use significant system resources, particularly when working with high-resolution footage and multiple effects. Check that your chosen application supports your macOS version and Mac processor. Keep enough free storage for source footage and exported videos.

Also review the free version's export resolution, supported formats, watermark policy, and premium features. These details can change between software releases, so check the developer's official website before starting an important project.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free video editor for Mac?

DaVinci Resolve is a strong choice for advanced editing, while iMovie is an excellent option for beginners who want a simple workflow.

Does Mac have a built-in free video editor?

Yes. Apple iMovie is available free for supported Mac devices and provides basic editing, titles, transitions, and audio features.

Can I edit 4K videos for free on a Mac?

Yes. Some free editors, including DaVinci Resolve and Shotcut, support 4K workflows. Actual performance depends on your Mac, footage format, and export settings.

Are free video editors good for YouTube?

Absolutely. You can use free software to trim footage, add titles, improve audio, apply transitions, and export videos for YouTube.

Do free video editors add watermarks?

It depends on the application and the features or assets used. Check the current free-plan conditions before exporting your final video.
