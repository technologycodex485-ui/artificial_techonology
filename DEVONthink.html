01 — INTRODUCTION

What Is DEVONthink for Mac?

DEVONthink is an advanced document management and knowledge organization application for Mac. It is designed for people who work with extensive collections of digital information, including PDFs, notes, research materials, web archives, and other documents.


Instead of keeping important information scattered across multiple folders and applications, users can build organized databases and manage their digital knowledge in a more structured way.

One Place for Your Information

From academic research and business documents to personal notes and reference materials, DEVONthink provides tools for maintaining an organized digital information library.
