What Is Perplexity AI?

Perplexity AI is an intelligent search and research platform that combines artificial intelligence with real-time web information. Instead of simply showing a list of search results, it analyzes relevant information and presents a summarized answer with links to supporting sources.

Is Perplexity AI Better Than Traditional Search Engines?

Perplexity AI offers a different search experience compared with traditional search engines. Traditional search engines primarily display links to websites, while Perplexity focuses on generating summarized answers with supporting citations.

This approach can be useful when users want a quick explanation or need to research a complicated subject. However, traditional search engines remain useful for discovering websites, products, local businesses, and a wide range of online resources.

The best option depends on your needs. Many users can benefit from using both approaches together.
