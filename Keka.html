What is Keka?

Keka is a cloud-based Human Resource Management System (HRMS) designed to help businesses manage their workforce more efficiently. It brings multiple HR activities together in one platform, helping organizations simplify everyday administrative tasks.

From employee information and payroll processing to attendance tracking and performance management, Keka provides tools that help HR professionals manage important workplace operations.

How Does Keka HR Software Work?

Keka provides a centralized platform where employees, managers and HR administrators can access relevant workforce information. Businesses can configure workflows, manage employee records and automate several routine HR processes.

Employees can use self-service features to access their information, submit leave requests and view relevant employment details, depending on their organization's configuration.

Important Features of Keka

  • Employee information and HR management.
  • Payroll processing and expense management.
  • Attendance and leave management.
  • Recruitment and employee onboarding.
  • Performance management and employee reviews.
  • Workforce reporting and analytics.

Benefits of Using Keka

One of the main advantages of an integrated HR platform is that it reduces the need to manage different HR tasks through disconnected systems. Keka helps organizations centralize employee information and streamline their internal workflows.

Its digital tools can also help reduce repetitive administrative work, improve access to employee information and support better coordination between HR teams, managers and employees.

Who Can Use Keka?

Keka can be useful for growing businesses, established organizations, HR departments, payroll administrators and managers who want a centralized approach to workforce management.

Conclusion

Keka is a comprehensive HR and payroll management solution that brings essential workforce processes together. Its combination of employee management, attendance, payroll, recruitment and performance tools makes it a platform worth exploring for organizations looking to modernize their HR operations.
