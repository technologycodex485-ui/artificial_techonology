01 / Introduction

What is CapCut?

A versatile video editor designed to make creative video production more accessible.


Visit Official CapCut ↗

CapCut is a video editing application developed by ByteDance. It gives users tools to trim clips, arrange footage, add transitions, create captions, adjust audio, and apply visual effects. Its approachable editing workflow makes it useful for people who are learning video production as well as experienced content creators.

One reason CapCut attracts attention is its availability across different platforms. Depending on the device and available features, users can edit through mobile apps, desktop software, or a web-based editor. This flexibility makes it useful for quick social media edits and more detailed video projects.

CapCut is particularly relevant to creators producing vertical videos, tutorials, product showcases, short-form entertainment, and promotional content. The tools available can vary by platform, region, app version, and subscription.

Who can benefit from CapCut?

Beginners can use its straightforward editing controls to learn the basics. Influencers and social media creators can build short videos with captions and effects. Small businesses can prepare product clips and promotional videos, while marketers can create content for different digital channels.

03 / Everyday workflows

What can you create with CapCut?

CapCut can support a wide range of video projects. The best workflow depends on your content, the device you use, and the result you want to achieve.

Social media videos

Create short vertical videos for platforms such as TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Combine concise clips, readable captions, music, and transitions to hold attention.

Product promotions

Put together product demonstrations, simple advertisements, unboxing videos, and promotional clips. Clear visuals and a focused message can help communicate product benefits.

Tutorials and educational content

Organize screen recordings, voiceovers, captions, and visual explanations into lessons that are easier for viewers to follow.

Personal and creative projects

Make travel edits, event highlights, photo slideshows, family videos, and other projects using footage from your own collection.

04 / Getting started

How to start editing with CapCut

Follow these basic steps to build your first video project.

Choose your platform

Open the mobile app, desktop editor, or official online editor that suits your workflow.

Import your footage

Select the video clips, photographs, and audio files you want to use in your project.

Edit and enhance

Trim clips, arrange the timeline, add captions, adjust sound, and apply suitable effects.

Preview and export

Review your video, select an available export setting, and save the finished file.

Tip: Start with a simple project. Focus on clear storytelling, readable text, balanced audio, and a consistent visual style before adding lots of effects.
05 / Plans & availability

Is CapCut free to use?

CapCut provides free editing options, with additional paid features available through subscription plans.

CapCut offers a free option with basic editing tools and selected templates. Users who need more advanced features, premium assets, additional AI capabilities, or expanded storage may consider a paid plan.

Subscription names, included features, AI credits, storage limits, and prices can change. The final price may also depend on your country, billing platform, account, and current offers.

Before subscribing, compare the current plans and check whether the tools you need are included. Casual editors may find the free option sufficient, while frequent creators may benefit from evaluating the paid features.

See the official CapCut pricing page for current plan information.

06 / Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about the CapCut video editor.

What is CapCut used for?

CapCut is used to edit videos, arrange clips, add captions, apply effects, adjust audio, and prepare content for social media and other video projects.

Is CapCut suitable for beginners?

Yes. Its accessible editing workflow and ready-made creative resources can help beginners learn basic video editing.

Can I use CapCut on a computer?

Yes. CapCut provides desktop editing software, as well as a browser-based editor. Check the official website for supported devices and current system requirements.

Does CapCut include AI features?

CapCut offers various AI-assisted tools. Availability and usage limits depend on the feature, platform, region, and subscription.

Is CapCut completely free?

CapCut has a free option, but some premium tools, assets, and services require a paid subscription or credits.

Can I make videos for YouTube and Instagram?

Yes. You can create videos for different platforms and choose suitable aspect ratios and export settings supported by your version of the editor.

Where can I find official CapCut information?

Visit the official CapCut website and its help center for product details, downloads, tutorials, and troubleshooting.
