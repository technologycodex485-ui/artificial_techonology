About Adobe Creative Cloud

A complete creative environment for modern creators

Adobe Creative Cloud is a subscription-based collection of creative applications, services and resources developed by Adobe. It brings together software for designing graphics, editing photographs, producing videos, creating illustrations and preparing professional documents.

Its well-known applications include Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, After Effects, Lightroom and InDesign. Each app supports a different part of the creative process, allowing professionals and beginners to choose tools suited to their work.

Creative Cloud also connects users with services such as Adobe Fonts, Creative Cloud Libraries, Behance and Adobe Firefly. Depending on the plan, users may also receive cloud storage, tutorials, templates and generative AI features.

Choose a Creative Cloud plan for your work

Single-App Plans

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Adobe Creative Cloud

Helpful answers about Adobe applications, subscriptions, installation and creative features.

1. What is Adobe Creative Cloud?

Adobe Creative Cloud is a collection of creative applications, online services and resources used for photography, graphic design, video editing, illustration, publishing and other creative tasks.

2. Which applications are included?

Depending on the subscription, Adobe offers applications such as Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, After Effects, Lightroom, InDesign, Acrobat Pro and Adobe Firefly.

3. Is Adobe Creative Cloud free?

Adobe offers some free tools and limited services, but many professional Creative Cloud applications require a paid subscription. Trial availability varies by product and eligibility.

4. Can I use Photoshop and Illustrator together?

Yes. Both applications can be used in the same creative workflow. Photoshop is commonly used for raster image editing, while Illustrator specializes in vector artwork.

5. Does Creative Cloud include video editing software?

Yes. Eligible Creative Cloud plans include video and motion-production applications such as Premiere and After Effects.

6. What is Adobe Firefly?

Adobe Firefly is Adobe's family of generative AI tools for creating and editing visual, video and audio content. Access and usage limits depend on the plan and feature.

7. Can I use Creative Cloud on multiple devices?

Adobe supports app installation and account access across compatible devices, subject to its activation, sign-in and device limits. Check the current licensing terms.

8. Does Creative Cloud work on Windows and Mac?

Many Adobe desktop applications are available for Windows and macOS. Exact operating-system and hardware requirements differ by application and version.

9. Is cloud storage included?

Many Creative Cloud subscriptions include cloud storage, but the amount and available services depend on the specific plan and account.

10. Who should use Adobe Creative Cloud?

It can be useful for graphic designers, photographers, video editors, illustrators, marketers, students, content creators and professional creative teams.
