Introduction

A Simpler Way to Follow Your Favorite Websites

Keeping up with online news can be challenging when you visit many websites every day. Important updates can get lost among browser tabs, social media posts, and email newsletters. An RSS reader offers another way to stay connected with the sources you enjoy.

NetNewsWire for Mac is a free and open-source RSS reader designed to help users follow website feeds and read articles from one application. It provides a focused reading environment for people who prefer choosing their own news sources.

From technology blogs and independent publishers to specialist news websites, NetNewsWire helps make following online publications more organized.
