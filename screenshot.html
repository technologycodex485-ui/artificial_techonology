Introduction to Taking Screenshots on Mac

Knowing how to screenshot on Mac is useful for students, professionals, designers, and everyday users. You can save important information, capture error messages, share instructions, or keep a visual record of something on your screen.

The good news is that you do not need to install additional software for basic screenshots. Your Mac has built-in keyboard shortcuts and a Screenshot application that make capturing your display simple.

1. How to Capture the Entire Screen on Mac

If you want to take a picture of everything currently visible on your display, use the full-screen screenshot shortcut.

Follow These Steps:

  1. Open the screen you want to capture.
  2. Press Command + Shift + 3 together.
  3. Your Mac captures the entire screen.
  4. Find the screenshot on your desktop by default.

This method is helpful when you need to save your complete desktop, an application window arrangement, or a full webpage view that fits on your display.

2. How to Screenshot a Selected Area

Sometimes you only need a small part of your screen. macOS lets you select exactly which area you want to save.

Steps to Capture a Selected Area:

  1. Press Command + Shift + 4.
  2. Your pointer changes into a crosshair.
  3. Click and drag to select the desired area.
  4. Release the mouse or trackpad to capture it.
Helpful Tip: After pressing Command + Shift + 4, press the Space bar to switch to window capture mode. Then select the window you want to capture.

3. How to Use the Mac Screenshot Toolbar

The Screenshot toolbar gives you more control over capturing your display. It is especially useful if you want to record your screen instead of taking a still image.

Open the Screenshot Toolbar

  1. Press Command + Shift + 5.
  2. Wait for the screenshot controls to appear.
  3. Choose full-screen, window, or selected-area capture.
  4. Alternatively, choose a screen-recording option.
  5. Click Capture or Record to begin.

You can also open the Options menu to choose a save destination, set a timer, or adjust other available screenshot settings.

4. Mac Screenshot Keyboard Shortcuts

Here is a quick reference table of useful macOS screenshot shortcuts.

Keyboard Shortcut Function
Command + Shift + 3 Capture the entire screen
Command + Shift + 4 Capture a selected area
Command + Shift + 4, then Space Capture a specific window
Command + Shift + 5 Open screenshot and recording controls
Command + Shift + 6 Capture the Touch Bar on supported Macs

5. Where Are Screenshots Saved on Mac?

By default, screenshots are saved to your desktop. They are generally stored as PNG image files.

If you want to change the save location, open the Screenshot toolbar using Command + Shift + 5, select Options, and choose an available destination.

Depending on your macOS version and settings, you may also be able to save screenshots to a folder, clipboard, or another supported location.

6. How to Copy a Screenshot to the Clipboard

If you want to paste a screenshot directly into an email, document, or messaging application without saving an image file, use the Control key with a screenshot shortcut.

  • Press Control + Command + Shift + 3 for the entire screen.
  • Press Control + Command + Shift + 4 for a selected area.
  • Paste the captured image into a compatible application.

7. Why Mac Screenshot Shortcuts May Not Work

If your screenshot shortcuts are not responding, check a few basic settings before trying again.

  • Check whether the keyboard is connected and working.
  • Open System Settings and check Keyboard shortcuts.
  • Look under the Screenshots shortcut settings.
  • Check whether another application is using the same shortcut.
  • Restart your Mac if the issue continues.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I screenshot on a MacBook quickly?

Press Command + Shift + 3 to capture the entire screen instantly.

How do I screenshot only part of my Mac screen?

Press Command + Shift + 4, then drag your pointer over the area you want to capture.

Can I record my screen on Mac?

Yes. Press Command + Shift + 5 and choose one of the screen-recording options.

Where do Mac screenshots go?

Screenshots are normally saved to the desktop unless you select a different destination in the Screenshot toolbar's Options menu.

Can I take a screenshot without saving it?

Yes. Hold Control along with the appropriate screenshot keyboard shortcut to copy the capture to your clipboard.
