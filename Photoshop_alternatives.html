Top Alternatives

7 Best Photoshop Alternatives for Mac

These applications offer different creative workflows. Some focus on detailed photo manipulation, while others are designed for illustration, simple graphic design, or quick browser-based editing.

01

Affinity Photo

Affinity Photo is a powerful image editing application designed for detailed photo adjustments, retouching, layer-based compositions, masking, and creative effects. It is a strong option for photographers and designers who want advanced editing capabilities. Check the developer's current Mac availability and licensing terms.

Advanced Photo Editing
02

GIMP

GIMP is a free, open-source image editor with layers, selections, masks, filters, and image manipulation tools. It is a useful alternative for users who want extensive editing features without a paid subscription.

Best Free Alternative
03

Pixelmator Pro

Pixelmator Pro provides a Mac-focused editing experience with photo adjustments, layer editing, retouching, and design tools. Its interface is designed to fit naturally into the Apple ecosystem. Check current availability and pricing before purchasing.

Best Mac-Focused Editor
04

Krita

Krita is an open-source creative application particularly suited to digital painting, illustration, brushes, and concept art. It also provides layers and image editing tools for artists who want a free creative workspace.

Best for Digital Artists
05

Photopea

Photopea is a browser-based image editor with a familiar layer-based interface. It supports many common image formats and can be useful for quick edits without installing a desktop application. Its free version may display advertisements.

Best Online Alternative
06

Canva

Canva is ideal for social media graphics, presentations, posters, thumbnails, and marketing designs. Its templates and simple editing tools make it approachable for beginners, although it is not a full replacement for advanced pixel-level photo manipulation.

Best for Graphic Design
07

Acorn

Acorn is a lightweight image editing application for Mac. It provides tools for layers, selections, shapes, filters, and photo adjustments. It can be suitable for users who prefer a straightforward desktop editing experience.

Best Lightweight Option

Frequently Asked Questions

Photoshop Alternatives FAQs

What is the best free Photoshop alternative for Mac?

GIMP is one of the strongest free desktop alternatives because it offers layers, masks, filters, and image manipulation tools. Krita is another excellent free option for digital artists.

Can I use Photoshop alternatives on a MacBook?

Yes. Many image editors support MacBook devices. Always check the application's system requirements and processor compatibility.

Is GIMP better than Photoshop?

GIMP can handle many image editing tasks, but Photoshop offers a different workflow, extensive professional integrations, and specialized features. The better option depends on your requirements.

Which alternative is easiest for beginners?

Canva is particularly beginner-friendly for graphic design. Pixelmator Pro and other simplified Mac editors can also provide approachable photo editing workflows.

Can I edit PSD files without Photoshop?

Some alternatives can open PSD files, but compatibility may vary. Complex layers, effects, fonts, and adjustment settings may not transfer perfectly between applications.
