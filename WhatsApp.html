What Is WhatsApp?

Discover an easy way to communicate and share moments with people around the world.

Simple and Convenient Communication

WhatsApp Messenger is a communication platform developed by Meta. It allows users to exchange text messages, photographs, videos, voice messages, and documents.

The application also supports voice and video calling, group conversations, status updates, and various privacy settings. Its straightforward interface makes it accessible to users with different levels of technical experience.

WhatsApp is available on supported Android and iOS devices, as well as desktop and web platforms.
