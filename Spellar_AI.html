What Is Spellar AI?

Spellar AI is an intelligent meeting assistant that helps individuals and professionals make their meetings more productive. Instead of spending valuable time writing every detail manually, users can record their conversations and use AI-powered tools to generate structured meeting notes.

Spellar supports platforms and devices including Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Chrome. One of its notable features is bot-free recording, meaning an additional meeting bot does not need to join your online conversation.

Quick Overview: Spellar AI combines meeting recording, transcription, AI summaries, action items, and intelligent meeting search in one productivity-focused workspace.
