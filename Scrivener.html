01 — INTRODUCTION

What Is Scrivener for Mac?

Scrivener is a comprehensive writing application developed for people working on large and complex writing projects. Unlike basic text editors, it combines writing, research, outlining, and document organization in one workspace.


Whether you are planning a novel, preparing an academic paper, developing a screenplay, or creating a detailed research document, Scrivener provides tools to manage different parts of your work without losing sight of the bigger picture.

"Great writing begins with organized ideas and a workspace that supports creativity."

03 — TARGET USERS

Who Can Benefit from Scrivener?

Scrivener is particularly useful for people who regularly work with lengthy documents and need more than a simple word processor.

  • Novelists and fiction writers
  • Bloggers and professional authors
  • Students and academic researchers
  • Screenwriters and storytellers
  • Content creators and journalists

A Better Way to Manage Large Projects

One of Scrivener's biggest advantages is its project-based workflow. You can work on separate sections, move content around, store research, and assemble everything into a complete document.


This makes it especially valuable when a writing project contains multiple chapters, drafts, references, and supporting materials.
