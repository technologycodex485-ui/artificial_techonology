What Is Spotify?

Learn about a popular audio streaming service that brings music, podcasts, and personalized listening experiences together.

A Complete Digital Music Experience

Spotify is a digital audio streaming service that allows users to explore and listen to a wide selection of music, podcasts, and other audio content. It provides access to artists, albums, playlists, and curated recommendations.

The platform offers personalized listening experiences based on users' musical interests. Its search tools and discovery features make it easier to find new songs and revisit favorites.

Spotify is available on supported smartphones, tablets, desktop computers, smart TVs, and other compatible devices. Both free and paid subscription options are available, with features varying by plan and region.
