What Is Rize?

Rize is a time-tracking application that helps professionals, freelancers, consultants, and teams understand where their work hours go. Rather than relying entirely on manual timers, the desktop application captures activity in the background and organizes it into reviewable time entries.

The software uses activity context and AI-assisted suggestions to help connect tracked time with clients, projects, and tasks. This can make time reporting easier and help people build a clearer picture of their daily work habits.

In simple terms: Rize helps turn everyday computer activity into organized time records, making it easier to review your workday and understand how your time is being used.

Main Features of Rize

Rize combines automatic activity capture with tools for reviewing work, improving focus, and understanding project time. Here are some of the features that make the platform useful.

⏱️

Automatic Time Tracking

Rize records active-window metadata in the background, helping users build a timeline without constantly starting and stopping timers.

🧠

AI Time Suggestions

AI can suggest descriptions and tags for time entries, helping organize activity around clients, projects, and tasks.

🎯

Focus Sessions

Focus tools help users structure uninterrupted work periods and understand patterns in their concentration.

📈

Reports and Insights

Time records and reporting tools can help users review workload, project allocation, and work patterns.

🔒

Privacy Controls

Rize tracks activity metadata rather than screenshots or keystrokes, with settings to customize tracked information.

👥

Team Workspaces

Team features help organize client work, project activity, reporting, and collaboration in a shared workspace.

How Does Rize Work?

Rize is designed to make time tracking a more natural part of the working day. After installation and setup, it can capture activity and help organize that information into useful records.

1. Install and Configure

Download the desktop application, sign in, and configure the permissions and tracking settings it requires.

2. Let Activity Tracking Run

Rize captures information about the active app, window title, available URL, and timestamps while tracking is enabled.

3. Review Time Entries

Check the generated time entries and review suggested descriptions, clients, projects, and task labels.

4. Learn from Your Reports

Use your time records and reports to understand work allocation and make more informed planning decisions.

Who Should Use Rize?

Rize can be useful for people who want more accurate time records without managing a manual timer throughout the day. Freelancers may use it to understand client work, while agencies and teams can use project-level records to review workloads and reporting.

Consultants, independent professionals, and business operators may also find it useful when they need a clearer picture of how their working hours are distributed.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Rize used for?

Rize is used for automatic time tracking, organizing work entries, understanding productivity patterns, and reviewing time spent on clients and projects.
