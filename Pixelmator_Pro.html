What Is Keka?

Keka is a modern human resource management and payroll software platform designed to help businesses manage their employees and organize everyday HR activities. It combines different workforce management functions in one digital platform.

Organizations use HR management software to simplify administrative tasks, organize employee information and improve the management of workplace processes. Keka provides tools for payroll, attendance, recruitment, employee management and performance tracking.

Quick Overview: Keka is an HR technology platform that supports businesses with employee management, payroll processing, attendance tracking and other workforce operations.

Why Do Businesses Use Keka?

As companies grow, managing employee information and HR activities can become more complicated. HR departments need to coordinate salaries, attendance, leave requests, hiring and employee reviews.

Keka brings various HR functions together, helping businesses manage their workforce through a centralized system. This approach can reduce repetitive administrative work and improve the organization of HR information.
