What Is Adobe Photoshop?

Discover one of the world's best-known creative applications and learn how it supports modern digital image editing.

Explore Professional Image Editing

Adobe Photoshop is a professional image editing and graphic design application developed by Adobe. It provides a comprehensive collection of tools for editing photographs, creating digital artwork, designing graphics, and enhancing images.

Photoshop offers features such as layers, masks, selection tools, filters, adjustment controls, brushes, typography, and advanced image manipulation. These capabilities allow users to make detailed changes to their creative projects.

Whether you are a beginner learning photo editing or an experienced designer working on complex compositions, Photoshop offers a flexible environment for creative work.

Who Can Use Adobe Photoshop?

A Creative Tool for Different Professionals

Photographers use Photoshop to enhance images and perform detailed retouching. Graphic designers use it to create advertisements, promotional graphics, banners, and digital artwork.

Digital artists can create illustrations and imaginative compositions, while content creators can prepare thumbnails, social media graphics, and other visual content.

Photoshop provides creative possibilities for beginners, freelancers, businesses, and experienced design professionals.
